# European Car Market Analysis (2012–2023)
## Project overview
This project analyzes changes in the European passenger car market between 2012 and 2023. The analysis focuses on market structure, bestselling models and the growth of SUV vehicles.
## Research questions
- How did the market structure change?
- Which segments gained or lost market share?
- To what extent do changes among the bestselling models reflect structural changes in the overall market?
- How did the rise of SUVs influence the market?
## Dataset
The project uses three datasets:
- S&P Global Mobility, published by ACEA – market segment data
- Best-selling-cars.com – annual bestselling model sales
- Wikipedia – vehicle generation and segment classification
Data sources:
- ACEA
- Best-selling-cars.com
- Wikipedia
## Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
## Key findings
- Overall market sales fluctuated considerably.
- The share of the Top 10 bestselling models declined over time.
- Segments C and D lost market share.
- SUVs grew to become the largest market segment.
- The growth of SUVs was stronger in the overall market than among the bestselling models.
- Changes among the Top 10 bestselling models reflected some major market trends, but their magnitude and timing differed from those observed in the overall market.
